Statutory Plan In Sabah

The Town and Country Planning Ordinance (Sabah Cap. 141) serves as the primary legislation governing town and country planning in Sabah.

This ordinance mandates two levels of statutory plans for Sabah. The first tier is the Structure Plan, which serves as the overarching plan for the entire state, including both the land and its territorial waters. It is prepared by the Director of the Sabah Town and Regional Planning Department on behalf of the State Planning Council and approved by the Cabinet under Section 4C(1).

GIS DATABASE DEVELOPMENT

Core spatial data domain definitions for urban master planning

Core spatial data domain for Urban Planning :
Administrative & Base Maps: Land boundaries, districts and census area.
Population & Socio-economic: Population numbers, density, households and income.
Land Use & Zoning: Current land use, zoning regulations and land ownership.
Transportation: Roads, public transport, traffic, and travel routes.
Environment & Green Areas: Parks, forests, rivers, flood risks and pollution.
Physical Terrain: Land Elevation, slopes, soil, geology and climate.
Utilities & Public Services: Water, electricity, drainage, hospitals, schools. Police and fire services.

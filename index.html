Statutory Plan In Sabah[cite: 1, 6, 7, 10]

The Town and Country Planning Ordinance (Sabah Cap. 141) serves as the primary legislation governing town and country planning in Sabah.[cite: 1, 6, 7, 10]

This ordinance mandates two levels of statutory plans for Sabah.[cite: 1, 6, 7, 10] The first tier is the Structure Plan, which serves as the overarching plan for the entire state, including both the land and its territorial waters.[cite: 1, 6, 7, 10] It is prepared by the Director of the Sabah Town and Regional Planning Department on behalf of the State Planning Council and approved by the Cabinet under Section 4C(1).[cite: 1, 6, 7, 10]

Why Is It Still 100% Manual? (The Challenge)

Urban planning is bound by legal and statutory requirements, not just visual satellite mapping! GeoAI output often struggles with irregular borders, overlapping polygons, or misalignment with actual survey lots. To meet planning standards, AI outputs must align with:

Cadastral Lot Boundaries

Precise legal geometry & title alignment.

Express Land Conditions

Statutory land use matching & titles.

Land Use Zoning

Defined under state planning acts & ordinances.

MODEL TRIAL & ERROR PHASES

Evolution across baseline architecture, deep encoders, and target goal

Phase 1: Baseline
Standard U-Net

Shows detected classes but boundaries are "blocky" and jagged. Too coarse for statutory planning needs.

Phase 2: Current Status
U-Net + ResNet34 + Overlap Patching

Current model limitations. Visual testing highlights the challenge of jagged vs sharp corners in cadastral alignment.

Phase 3: Goal Benchmark
U-Net++ + ResNet50 + TTA

Ultimate goal for maximum precision & statutory alignment (Currently in gradual development due to long training times).

05

CURRENT MODEL LIMITATIONS (KEY FINDINGS)

Critical geometrical discrepancies identified during testing

While the U-Net and ResNet models successfully detect land use classes overall, visual analysis reveals significant limitations in the current phase:

  • Boundary Detection Issues: Object boundary lines often appear wavy, coarse, and misaligned with actual cadastral lots.
  • Corner Accuracy (Sharp vs. Jagged Corners): The model struggles to maintain sharp corners at lot angles, resulting in jagged vertices that require complex manual geometric cleaning processes.
06

CONCLUSION & FUTURE RECOMMENDATIONS

Next steps for automated statutory parcel generation

The integration of GeoAI into land use mapping demonstrates immense potential to drastically reduce manual digitization bottlenecks in urban planning workflows. However, bridging the gap between raw pixel classification and statutory cadastral compliance remains a critical challenge.

Key Takeaways & Next Steps:
  • • 1. Advanced Architecture Transition: Moving towards deeper encoders like ResNet50 and U-Net++ combined with Test-Time Augmentation (TTA).
  • • 2. Post-Processing Automation: Integrating automated topology rules and Douglas-Peucker geometric simplification algorithms.
  • • 3. Scalable Enterprise Deployment: Transitioning the pipeline from Google Colab notebooks to scalable cloud-native architectures (GEE & PostGIS).

Statutory Plan In Sabah

The Town and Country Planning Ordinance (Sabah Cap. 141) serves as the primary legislation governing town and country planning in Sabah.

This ordinance mandates two levels of statutory plans for Sabah. The first tier is the Structure Plan, which serves as the overarching plan for the entire state, including both the land and its territorial waters. It is prepared by the Director of the Sabah Town and Regional Planning Department on behalf of the State Planning Council and approved by the Cabinet under Section 4C(1).

Core spatial data domain for Urban Planning :

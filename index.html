Sabah Cap. 141 Statutory Hierarchy[cite: 1, 6, 7]

The Town and Country Planning Ordinance (Sabah Cap. 141) provides the authoritative legal architecture for physical planning and state-wide territorial development[cite: 1, 6, 7]:

Tier 1 - Structure Plan: Overarching macro plan for the state and territorial waters, prepared by the Director of Town and Regional Planning for the State Planning Council and endorsed by Cabinet under Section 4C(1)[cite: 1, 6, 7].

Tier 2 - Local Plan: Detailed micro-level zoning blueprints regulating land parcel rights, road alignments, density standards, and public facilities reservations.

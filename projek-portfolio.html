📊 Project Overview

This project demonstrates data-driven spatial intelligence for agricultural strategic planning, analyzing idle land distributions across Kota Belud to identify high-potential paddy expansion opportunities using advanced GIS techniques and GeoAI.

🎯 Business Objective

For agricultural agencies and town planners (e.g., Jabatan Pertanian, SMART SBB BERNAS), current site selection strategy is often reactive — relying on manual field surveys without quantifying actual spatial parameters. This analysis provides a proactive, evidence-based approach to site selection.

Key Goals:

  1. Identify spatial suitability — quantify where optimal physical factors concentrate (soil, slope, hydrology)
  2. Analyze current land use — map service areas of existing active paddy fields
  3. Find agricultural gaps — locate high-potential, unutilized zones ("white space" / tanah terbiar)
  4. Recommend expansion sites — rank optimal locations with suitability opportunity scores

💼 Market Context & Why This Matters

Sabah Agricultural Food Security Opportunity

  • National focus: Increasing the Self-Sufficiency Level (SSL) for local rice production (2025-2030)
  • High idle land prevalence: Extensive abandoned agricultural plots that require systematic revitalization
  • Technological transition: Shift towards precise, drone/satellite-based monitoring to reduce manual surveying costs
  • Geospatial limitation: Complex topography requires accurate modeling and professional spatial consultation

Why Kota Belud?

  • ✓ Designated national granary area (Jelapang Padi) = high concentration of agricultural initiatives
  • ✓ Existing irrigation infrastructure (IADA) = crucial for sustainable paddy cultivation
  • ✓ Diverse topographic landscape = excellent for testing multi-criteria spatial models
  • ✓ Manageable district size = feasible for detailed high-resolution spatial analysis

⚙️ Key Technical Concepts

1. Multi-Criteria Decision Making (MCDM-AHP)

  • Definition: Mathematical framework to evaluate and assign weights to spatial criteria (elevation, drainage, soil type)
  • Advantage over simple overlay: Provides a scientifically validated consistency ratio for decision prioritization
  • Business application: Define "Highly Suitable" (intervention zones) vs "Unsuitable" areas

2. Semantic Segmentation (GeoAI U-Net)

  • Technique: Automated landcover classification from high-resolution imagery using PyTorch Deep Learning
  • Advantage: Identifies precise field boundaries accounting for spatial noise (<30 sqm filtering) via ArcPy

📈 Analysis Steps & Key Findings

Step 1: Data Collection & Preparation

  • Loaded physical parameters from GIS databases (DEM, Soil, River buffers)
  • Extracted agricultural indicators via GeoAI segmentation:
    • Active paddy fields: Mapped using U-Net model on Google Colab (T4 GPU)
    • Idle lands (Tanah terbiar): 50+ potential unutilized sites identified
    • Water sources: Distance to nearest irrigation networks mapped
    • Total suitability zones: Derived from AHP weighted overlay
